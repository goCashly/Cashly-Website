Why Consolidate

Why rolling debt into your mortgage can help, when it's structured right.

Consolidation isn't automatically the right move. Here's what we weigh before recommending it.

01 High-interest debt compounds quickly

Credit card and unsecured debt typically carries a much higher rate than mortgage-secured financing.

02 One payment is easier to track

Replacing several due dates and balances with a single mortgage payment reduces the chance of a missed payment.

03 Secured debt is usually priced lower

Debt secured against your home typically carries a lower rate than unsecured credit cards or lines of credit.

04 A plan matters more than the consolidation itself

Consolidation works best paired with a realistic plan so the same debt doesn't re-accumulate.

Debt Consolidation FAQ

Questions about mortgage debt consolidation in Ontario.

What is debt consolidation through a private mortgage?

It means rolling high-interest debt, such as credit cards, loans, or tax arrears, into your mortgage, secured against your home equity, so you make one payment instead of several.

Will consolidating my debt into a mortgage affect my credit?

Paying off revolving high-interest balances can improve your credit utilization over time, though any new financing involves a credit check. A licensed broker can walk through what to expect for your specific file.

How much debt can I consolidate?

That depends on your property's equity position and the lender. We review your current mortgage balance, property value, and total debt to see what's realistic.

Is debt consolidation the same as a second mortgage?

Debt consolidation is a purpose, not a structure; it can be arranged through either a refinance of your first mortgage or a second mortgage behind it, depending on which fits your situation better.

What if I have bad credit or a consumer proposal?

Private lenders weigh home equity more heavily than credit score, so a consumer proposal or credit event doesn't automatically rule out debt consolidation financing.

Carrying more than one high-interest balance?

Tell us what debts you're trying to consolidate and we'll help you understand whether your equity position makes it realistic.
