Why Private, Not Bank

Why the bank said no, and why that isn't the whole story.

A decline on a purchase file is usually a rule you didn't fit, not a reflection of the deal itself.

01 Banks lend on income and credit, not equity

Lender qualification rules require banks to assess declared income, credit history, and the stress test, even on a strong purchase.

02 A credit event doesn't erase your down payment

Consumer proposals, bankruptcy discharges, and low scores disqualify most bank applications outright.

03 Self-employed write-offs work against you

Business owners who legitimately minimize taxable income often show a return that understates real buying power.

04 Some closings can't wait on bank underwriting

An expiring purchase agreement doesn't leave room for a multi-week bank process.

Private Purchase Mortgage FAQ

Questions about private purchase financing in Ontario.

What is a private purchase mortgage?

A private purchase mortgage is financing for a home purchase funded by a private lender rather than a bank, approved primarily on the property and down payment rather than bank-style income and credit review.

Can self-employed buyers qualify for a private purchase mortgage?

Yes. Private lenders typically weigh the property and down payment more heavily than declared income, which often works better for self-employed buyers than a bank's documentation requirements.

How fast can a private purchase mortgage close?

Because approval turns on the property rather than a lengthy income-verification process, a private purchase file can often move in days once the appraisal and legal work are in motion.

What types of properties qualify?

Private lenders are often able to finance unique, rural, or non-standard properties that don't fit a bank's lending criteria, in addition to standard residential properties.

Do I need a large down payment for a private purchase mortgage?

Down payment requirements vary by lender and property. Because approval is equity-based, your down payment and the property's value matter more than income documentation.
